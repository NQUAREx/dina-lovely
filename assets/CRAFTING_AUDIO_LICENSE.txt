Processing:
pick_stone.ogg: short damped metal contact (65ms, resonant tail removed),
concrete body, quieter delayed grit and twig transient. No sustained bell.
chop_wood.ogg / axe.wav: compressed wood impact with close twig crack.
craft_bench.wav: three spaced wood/tool contacts, wood movement/scrapes,
final seating crack; 0.98s, matching the existing crafting task duration.
Outputs normalized to peak0.89 before Vorbis encoding; mono44.1kHz.
